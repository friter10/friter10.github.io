Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

rclone-kopia is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google Drive solely to store and retrieve encrypted backups of the owner's files.

No data is collected, shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party. No analytics or advertising tools are used. Google user data is used only to read and write backup files in the owner's own Drive.
